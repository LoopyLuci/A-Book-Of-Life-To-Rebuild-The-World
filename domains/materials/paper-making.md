# Paper Making

> **Domain:** materials | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Paper preserves knowledge. It is made from cellulose fiber (rag, hemp, straw)
beaten into pulp, then pressed and dried.

## Prerequisites

- [Rope & Cordage](../survival/rope-cordage.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Fiber: linen rag, cotton, hemp, straw
- Water
- Wooden beater or mortar
- Mold and deckle (wooden frame with mesh)
- Press
- Sizing (gelatin or starch)

## Procedure

### Preparing Pulp

1. Cut rags into 2 cm squares.
2. Ret in water 24 hours.
3. Beat with a wooden mallet or in a mortar to separate fibers.
4. Dilute in water to 1% consistency.

### Forming Sheets

1. Dip mold into pulp; lift horizontally.
2. Shake side-to-side to interlock fibers.
3. Drain; couch sheet onto felt.
4. Stack sheets between felts.

### Pressing & Drying

1. Press stack to remove water.
2. Hang sheets to dry on a line or board.
3. Dried sheets are brittle; size them.

### Sizing

1. Dip in warm gelatin (3%) or starch solution.
2. Dry. Sized paper resists ink bleed.

### Recycled Paper

1. Tear waste paper; soak 24 hours.
2. Blend in water; strain.
3. Proceed as above; fibers are shorter — mixed sheets may be weaker.

## Safety Notes

- Alkaline pulp can irritate skin — wear gloves for long sessions.
- Mold growth on drying sheets is possible in humid climates; dry thoroughly.
- Do not use treated or painted wood scraps for pulp.
