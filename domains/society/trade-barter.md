# Trade & Barter

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources

## Overview

Barter and local exchange move goods without money. Fair terms, standard
measures, and reputation keep trade flowing and prevent exploitation.

## Prerequisites

- Economics

## Materials & Tools

- Goods to trade
- Standard weights and measures
- Market rules

## Procedure

### Negotiate

1. Know the value of what you offer and seek.
2. Agree terms before exchanging, ideally with witnesses.

### Local Currency

1. Issue scrip or tally sticks backed by real goods or labor.
2. Track balances to prevent inflation.

## Safety Notes

- Prevent fraud with standard measures and public records.
