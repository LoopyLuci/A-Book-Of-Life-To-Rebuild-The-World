# Conflict Resolution

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Unresolved conflict destroys communities. Mediation that addresses interests, not positions, restores relationships and prevents violence.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Neutral meeting space
- Trained mediators
- Written agreement form

## Procedure

### Mediation

1. Separate parties to cool emotions if needed.
2. Have each side state interests without interruption.
3. Brainstorm options and select a mutually acceptable solution.

### Restoration

1. Agree on restitution or repair.
2. Record the agreement and follow up.

## Safety Notes

- Separate parties where violence is likely; involve the community for safety.
