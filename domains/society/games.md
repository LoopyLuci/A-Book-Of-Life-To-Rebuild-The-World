# Games

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Games transmit strategy, math, and social skills. They are played for joy,
teaching, and community bonding.

## Prerequisites

- [Festivals & Celebration](festivals-celebration.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Dice, stones, sticks, boards, cards
- Two or more players

## Procedure

### Dice

- **Knucklebones:** animal ankle bones.
- **Cubical dice:** carved from wood, bone, or clay.
- **Games:** craps, yatzy, liars' dice.

### Mancala

1. Board with 2 rows of 6 pits + 2 stores.
2. Each pit starts with 4 stones.
3. Sow stones counter-clockwise.
4. Capture opponent's stones.
5. Win by accumulating the most.

### Tigers & Goats

1. 4 tigers vs 20 goats on a 5×5 grid.
2. Goats block; tigers jump to capture.
3. Tigers win by eating 5 goats.
4. Goats win by blocking all tigers.

### Chess

1. 8×8 board.
2. Each side: 8 pawns, 2 rooks, 2 knights, 2 bishops, 1 queen, 1 king.
3. Objective: checkmate the king.
4. Teaches strategy, patience, foresight.

### Go

1. 19×19 board (or 13×13, 9×9 for beginners).
2. Players place black/white stones alternately.
3. Surround territory to capture.
4. Objective: control more territory than opponent.
5. Oldest continuously-played board game.

### Playing Cards

1. 52-card deck (4 suits × 13 ranks).
2. Games: poker, bridge, whist, rummy.
3. Card games teach probability.

### The Game of Life (Simulation)

1. Board with life path.
2. Spin to advance.
3. Choices: career, family, wealth.
4. Teaches consequences.

### Physical Games

- Tag, hide-and-seek, races.
- Wrestling, tug-of-war.
- Ball games: soccer, catch, juggling.

### Teaching Games

- Counting games for children.
- Memory games.
- Strategy games for teens.

### Gambling

- Historically common but harmful.
- If played, keep stakes symbolic.
- Never gamble essentials.

## Safety Notes

- Green — but gambling addiction destroys families. Avoid real stakes.
- Physical games risk injury; warm up and play fairly.
- Chess and Go can become obsessive; balance.
