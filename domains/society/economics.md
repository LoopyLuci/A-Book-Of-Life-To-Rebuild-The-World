# Economics

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Economics allocates scarce resources. Tracking production, storage, and exchange prevents both shortage and waste.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Ledger
- Weights and measures
- Market space

## Procedure

### Accounting

1. Record production, consumption, and stores.
2. Balance the books regularly.

### Exchange

1. Set fair prices or exchange rates.
2. Maintain trust through standard measures and honest weights.

## Safety Notes

- Prevent hoarding and fraud through transparent records.
