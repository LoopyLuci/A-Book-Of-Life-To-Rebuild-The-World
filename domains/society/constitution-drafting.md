## Overview

A constitution fixes the structure of governance, rights, and limits of power.
Short, clear, and amendable texts endure best.

## Prerequisites

- Legal Frameworks

## Materials & Tools

- Drafting committee
- Records of debate
- Ratification process

## Procedure

### Draft

1. State purpose, rights, and structure of government.
2. Define amendment and review procedures.

### Ratify

1. Debate publicly and record objections.
2. Adopt by broad consent and publish the text.

## Safety Notes

- Require supermajority for amendment to protect stability.
