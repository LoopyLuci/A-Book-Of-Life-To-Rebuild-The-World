# Consensus Decision-Making

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Consensus builds decisions that people will uphold. A clear process separates proposal, discussion, objections, and consent.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Facilitator
- Note-taker
- Agenda

## Procedure

### Proposal & Discussion

1. State the proposal clearly and its rationale.
2. Clarify questions before opinions.

### Objections & Consent

1. Seek principled objections, not vetoes for preference.
2. Amend to address concerns; test for consent.
3. Fall back to a vote only when consensus fails repeatedly.

## Safety Notes

- Protect minority voices; prevent domination by the loudest.
