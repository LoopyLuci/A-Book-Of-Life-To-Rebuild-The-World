# Ethics

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Ethics guides hard choices, especially triage and resource allocation. Shared principles make decisions defensible and reduce resentment.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Discussion circle
- Case examples
- Written principles

## Procedure

### Principles

1. Agree on core values: dignity, fairness, non-harm, stewardship.
2. Apply consistently across members.

### Triage

1. Prioritize by urgency and likelihood of benefit.
2. Document reasoning to maintain trust.

## Safety Notes

- Protect the vulnerable; avoid decisions made from fear or bias.
