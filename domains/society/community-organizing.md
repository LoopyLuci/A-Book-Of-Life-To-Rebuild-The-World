# Community Organizing

> **Domain:** society | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Communities solve problems when they organize. Mapping assets, building teams, and acting on shared goals turn scattered individuals into capable groups.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- List of members and skills
- Meeting schedule
- Shared task board

## Procedure

### Map & Connect

1. List people, skills, and resources.
2. Identify leaders and trusted connectors.

### Act

1. Choose a concrete, winnable goal.
2. Assign roles, set dates, and review progress.

## Safety Notes

- Include marginalized members; distributed leadership resists burnout.
