# Bicycle Maintenance and Load Carrying

> **Domain:** transport | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

A bicycle is the most efficient human transport available: 10-30 km a day
unloaded, or 20-50 kg with a rack and low gearing. Most breakdowns are simple:
a punctured tube, a stretched brake cable, a dry chain, or a loose spoke.
Learning to fix these keeps a household mobile when fuel and spare parts are
scarce.

## Prerequisites

- A bicycle that fits the rider
- Basic hand tools and a pump

## Materials & Tools

- Spare inner tubes and a patch kit
- Tyre levers and a hand pump
- Chain lubricant and a rag
- Brake pads and cable
- Spoke wrench
- Rack, panniers, and a cargo net
- Helmet and lights

## Procedure

### 1. Do a pre-ride check

1. Squeeze the tyres; inflate to the pressure marked on the sidewall.
2. Test both brakes: the lever should not reach the handlebar.
3. Check that the wheels spin straight and the quick releases are closed.
4. Lubricate the chain and wipe off excess; check for loose racks and bolts.

### 2. Repair a puncture

1. Remove the wheel and let out the remaining air.
2. Use tyre levers to lift the tyre bead, then remove the tube.
3. Find the puncture by inflating the tube and listening or looking for bubbles.
4. Patch or replace the tube, check the tyre for the thorn or glass that caused
   it, and refit without pinching the tube.

### 3. Adjust brakes and gears

1. Replace worn pads before they reach the wear line.
2. Set pad clearance so the wheel spins freely but the brake bites firmly.
3. Check cable tension and replace frayed cables.
4. Index the gears so the chain shifts without overshooting.

### 4. Carry loads safely

1. Fit a rack to the frame, not to the seat post, for loads over 10 kg.
2. Keep weight low and centred; avoid hanging bags from the handlebar.
3. Use panniers or a cargo net so loose items cannot reach the wheel or chain.
4. Ride slower, brake earlier, and use low gears on hills.

## Safety Notes

- **Yellow hazard:** Brake or tyre failure in traffic can be fatal. Inspect
  before every ride.
- Use a helmet, lights, and reflective clothing; assume drivers have not seen
  you.
- Keep fingers and loose clothing away from the chain, disc brakes, and spokes.
- Drink water and rest in heat; load carrying raises exertion quickly.
