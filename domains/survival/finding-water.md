# Finding Water

> **Domain:** survival | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** yellow

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Humans die in 3 days without water. Before treating water, you must find it.
Sources ranked by safety: springs > rainwater > flowing streams > lakes >
stagnant pools.

## Prerequisites

- [Water Purification](water-purification.md) (all found water must be treated)

## Materials & Tools

- Container
- Cloth (for pre-filtering)
- Solar still materials (plastic sheet, cup, vegetation)

## Procedure

### Reading the Landscape

1. Follow game trails downhill — they lead to water.
2. Watch for swarms of insects at dusk (water nearby).
3. Look for green vegetation in arid terrain (indicates subsurface water).
4. Dig in dry riverbeds at the outside of bends.

### Solar Still

1. Dig a pit 1 m wide, 60 cm deep.
2. Place a cup in the center.
3. Fill the pit with green vegetation around the cup.
4. Cover with clear plastic; seal edges with soil.
5. Place a small stone at the center above the cup.
6. Collect condensed water after 6–8 hours.

### Vegetation Transpiration Bag

1. Tie a clear plastic bag around a leafy branch of a non-toxic tree.
2. Seal the opening; weight the bottom corner.
3. Collect transpired water after a day.

### Rain Catchment

1. Stretch cloth or plastic to funnel rain into a container.
2. Line large leaves (banana, taro) into a trough.
3. First 10 minutes of rain washes the surface — discard.

## Safety Notes

- **Never drink untreated water** — see [Water Purification](water-purification.md).
- Avoid water with algae blooms, oil sheen, or dead animals upstream.
- Desert plants may hold water but many are toxic; identify before drinking.
- Snow and ice must be melted — eating it causes hypothermia.
