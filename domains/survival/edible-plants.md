# Edible & Inedible Plants

> **Domain:** survival | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** red

## Overview

Plants provide calories, vitamins, and medicine — but misidentification kills.
Follow the **Universal Edibility Test** only in true survival situations. When
possible, rely on positive identification.

## Prerequisites

- [Finding Water](finding-water.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Plant samples
- Water for boiling
- Time (test takes 24 hours per plant)

## Procedure

### Positive Identification First

Learn 10–20 plants in your bioregion with absolute certainty before an
emergency. Prioritize:

- Dandelion (all parts edible)
- Plantain (leaves, seeds)
- Cattail (rhizomes, shoots, pollen)
- Clover (leaves, flowers)
- Acorns (leached of tannins)

### Universal Edibility Test (Last Resort)

1. **Separate** the plant into leaves, stem, root, bud, flower.
2. **Smell** each part; discard anything with an almond, peach, or bitter smell.
3. **Skin contact:** rub a small piece on inner wrist; wait 15 minutes.
4. **Lip contact:** place on lips; wait 15 minutes.
5. **Tongue:** place on tongue; wait 15 minutes.
6. **Chew:** chew a small piece and spit; wait 15 minutes.
7. **Swallow:** swallow one small piece; wait 8 hours.
8. If no reaction, eat ¼ cup and wait another 8 hours.

### Dangerous Families (Never Eat Unless Expert)

- Apiaceae (hemlock, water hemlock) — carrot family, deadly
- Solanaceae (nightshade, datura) — many toxic alkaloids
- Euphorbiaceae (spurge) — milky sap burns
- Mushrooms — unless 100% identified, never

### Preparation

- Boil most wild greens twice, discarding water, to remove oxalates and tannins.
- Leach acorns in cold water for 3 days, changing water twice daily.
- Cook cattail rhizomes; peel fibrous outer layer.

## Safety Notes

- **Red hazard:** Misidentification can be fatal within hours.
- Never eat plants with milky sap, umbrella flowers, or three-leaf patterns.
- Never eat mushrooms based on a book or app alone.
- Keep a charcoal slurry on hand in case of poisoning (see [Charcoal Production](../materials/charcoal-production.md)).
