# Antibiotics

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** red

## Overview

Antibiotics cure bacterial infection. Knowing which drug for which bug, and completing the full course, prevents resistance and relapse.

## Prerequisites

- Wound Care

## Materials & Tools

- Antibiotic stocks
- Clean water
- Dosing chart

## Procedure

### Selection

1. Match drug to likely organism and site.
2. Prefer narrow-spectrum agents when the target is known.

### Administration

1. Give the full course at correct intervals and dose.
2. Adjust for weight, age, pregnancy, and kidney function.

### Resistance

1. Never use antibiotics for viral illness.
2. Rotate and reserve drugs to slow resistance.

## Safety Notes

- Red hazard: allergies (especially penicillin) can be fatal.
- Overuse breeds resistant organisms that threaten the whole community.
