# Wound Care

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

Prompt cleaning and dressing prevents infection, the leading killer of field wounds. Control bleeding, remove debris, and keep the wound moist and covered.

## Prerequisites

- Basic First Aid

## Materials & Tools

- Clean water
- Soap
- Dressing and bandage
- Honey or antiseptic

## Procedure

### Cleaning

1. Control bleeding with direct pressure.
2. Irrigate the wound with clean, preferably boiled, water.
3. Remove visible dirt and dead tissue.

### Dressing

1. Apply a thin layer of honey or antiseptic for superficial wounds.
2. Cover with a clean non-stick dressing and change daily.

### Monitoring

1. Watch for spreading redness, pus, fever, or foul smell.
2. Escalate to antibiotic treatment if infection develops.

## Safety Notes

- Never close a contaminated or bite wound tightly.
- Tetanus prevention matters for all puncture wounds.
