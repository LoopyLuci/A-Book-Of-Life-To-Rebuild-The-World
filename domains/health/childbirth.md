# Childbirth

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** red

## Overview

Most births need only patience, warmth, cleanliness, and support. Recognizing obstructed labor and hemorrhage prevents the two leading causes of maternal death.

## Prerequisites

- Anatomy Basics

## Materials & Tools

- Clean cloths
- Sterile scissors or blade
- Cord ties
- Warm, clean space

## Procedure

### Labor Support

1. Keep the mother warm, hydrated, and calm.
2. Monitor contractions and the baby's descent.

### Delivery

1. Support the perineum and deliver the head slowly.
2. Check for a cord around the neck and clear the airway.
3. Deliver the body, then clamp and cut the cord.

### Aftercare

1. Deliver the placenta and watch for heavy bleeding.
2. Keep mother and newborn warm and begin breastfeeding.

## Safety Notes

- Red hazard: heavy bleeding, fever, or prolonged labor requires skilled help.
- Uterotonic medication and manual pressure control postpartum hemorrhage.
