# Mental Health

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Disaster and isolation cause acute stress, grief, and depression. Safety, connection, routine, and dignity are the first-line treatments.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Quiet space
- Water and food
- Daily routine plan

## Procedure

### Psychological First Aid

1. Ensure safety and basic needs.
2. Listen without judgment; do not force retelling.
3. Connect people to family and community.

### Ongoing Support

1. Establish routine, sleep, and physical activity.
2. Offer meaningful work and social roles.
3. Refer severe cases with risk of self-harm for specialist care.

## Safety Notes

- Never leave someone at acute risk of self-harm alone.
- Avoid debriefing that forces recounting of trauma.
