# Dental Care

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Dental pain disables and dental infection can spread fatally. Prevention by
cleaning and diet comes first; extraction is the reliable field cure.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Toothbrush or chew stick
- Fluoride or salt
- Dental instruments

## Procedure

### Prevention

1. Brush or chew clean sticks after meals.
2. Limit sugar; encourage fibrous foods.

### Pain & Abscess

1. Apply cold compresses and analgesics.
2. Drain a fluctuant abscess and give antibiotics if systemic.

### Extraction

1. Numb the area and loosen the tooth with elevators.
2. Remove smoothly, then pack the socket for a clean clot.

## Safety Notes

- Never extract an infected tooth with airway involvement; refer urgently.
- Manage bleeding and shock after extraction.
