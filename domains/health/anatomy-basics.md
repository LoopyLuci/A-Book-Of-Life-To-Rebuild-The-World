# Anatomy Basics

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Knowing the body's systems and landmarks guides examination, first aid, and
referral. Focus on the skeleton, major vessels, organs, and the airway.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Anatomical charts (public domain)
- Notebook
- Skeleton model (optional)

## Procedure

### Systems Overview

1. Learn the skeletal, muscular, circulatory, respiratory, digestive, and
   nervous systems.
2. Memorize the positions of the heart, lungs, liver, spleen, and kidneys.

### Landmarks

1. Palpate pulse points: radial, carotid, femoral.
2. Identify the airway, cricoid, and sternal notch.

## Safety Notes

- Anatomy knowledge supports, but does not replace, trained care.
