# Spring Development and Protection

> **Domain:** water | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

A spring is a place where groundwater reaches the surface. Development means
capturing that flow cleanly instead of letting it pool in mud. A protected
spring box, a sealed collection pipe, and a fence around the catchment turn a
seep into a supply that can feed a gravity pipeline for years.

## Prerequisites

- A site visit in the dry season to measure flow
- Permission for the land and water use
- Masonry and pipe-laying skills

## Materials & Tools

- Stone or concrete for the box
- Gravel and sand filter pack
- Collection pipe and overflow pipe
- Clay or mortar for sealing
- Cover slab and fence posts
- Downstream trough or break-pressure tank

## Procedure

### 1. Survey the spring

1. Locate the true source, not the downstream seepage.
2. Measure flow in the dry season with a container and stopwatch.
3. Dig test pits upslope to find the water-bearing layer.
4. Check upstream land use for latrines, livestock, or chemical storage.

### 2. Excavate and build the box

1. Open a stable pit below the spring eye, shoring loose ground.
2. Build a watertight box with an open bottom that sits in the aquifer.
3. Pack clean gravel around the collection area and seal the box walls against
   surface water.
4. Slope the floor slightly toward the collection pipe.

### 3. Install the outlet

1. Fit a screened collection pipe near the bottom.
2. Fit a larger overflow pipe so excess water leaves without washing out the
   structure.
3. Pipe the water to a covered tank or standpipe; keep the line buried and free
   of low points that can trap air.
4. Seal the cover slab and fit an inspection hatch.

### 4. Protect the catchment

1. Fence at least 10-30 m around the spring, upslope.
2. Keep animals and latrines out of the immediate catchment.
3. Plant deep-rooted vegetation to stabilize the slope.
4. Inspect after storms and repair erosion before it reaches the box.

## Safety Notes

- **Yellow hazard:** Excavation can collapse. Never work alone in a deep pit
  and shore the walls before entering.
- Spring water is not automatically safe. Test it and disinfect if it is used
  for drinking.
- Do not dam or divert a spring that supplies downstream users without
  agreement; water rights and livelihoods are at stake.
- Cover the box to prevent drowning and contamination.
