# Education

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Education transmits skills and values across generations. A curriculum built on literacy, numeracy, and apprenticeship rebuilds capability quickly.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Slates or paper
- Curriculum outline
- Trained instructors

## Procedure

### Foundations

1. Teach reading and writing first, then arithmetic.
2. Use local materials, stories, and practical problems.

### Apprenticeship

1. Pair learners with skilled practitioners.
2. Progress from observation to supervised work to independent practice.

### Assessment

1. Check understanding with practical tasks.
2. Adjust instruction to close gaps.

## Safety Notes

- Safeguard children; keep learning environments safe and inclusive.
