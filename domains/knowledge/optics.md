# Optics

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

Optics magnify, focus, and direct light, enabling reading aids, telescopes, microscopes, and fire-starting. Grinding and polishing produce lenses from glass.

## Prerequisites

- Glass Making

## Materials & Tools

- Glass blanks
- Abrasives and pitch
- Polishing rouge

## Procedure

### Grinding

1. Rough-grind the blank against a tool with coarse abrasive.
2. Progress to finer abrasives until the curve is correct.

### Polishing

1. Polish on pitch with rouge until clear.
2. Test focus on a bright object.

### Use

1. Combine lenses into a telescope or microscope.
2. Focus sunlight for ignition.

## Safety Notes

- Never view the sun directly through optics; it causes blindness.
- Focused sunlight can start fires instantly.
