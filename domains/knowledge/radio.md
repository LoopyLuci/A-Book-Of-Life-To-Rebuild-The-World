## Overview

Radio links communities beyond line of sight, using no infrastructure. A simple
crystal set receives; a low-power transceiver sends voice or Morse.

## Prerequisites

- Electrical Wiring

## Materials & Tools

- Copper wire
- Variable capacitor and coil
- Crystal earpiece or diode
- Antenna wire

## Procedure

### Receiver

1. Wind a coil and connect it to a variable capacitor to tune.
2. Add a diode detector and crystal earpiece.
3. Connect a long wire antenna and a good earth.

### Antenna

1. Run a half-wave dipole as high and clear as possible.
2. Feed with twin lead or coax and seal connections.

### Transmission

1. Use low power and a licensed band.
2. Send Morse or voice; identify your station.

## Safety Notes

- Powerful transmitters and antennas can cause RF burns and shocks.
- Follow local spectrum rules and keep emergency frequencies clear.
