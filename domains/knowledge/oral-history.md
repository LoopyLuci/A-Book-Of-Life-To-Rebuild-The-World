# Oral History

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

When writing is scarce, oral history preserves memory, law, and craft. Structured interviews and mnemonic devices keep it accurate.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Notebook or recording device
- Question guides
- Storytellers

## Procedure

### Collecting

1. Interview elders with open questions and permission.
2. Record exact wording and context.

### Preserving

1. Transcribe and cross-check with other accounts.
2. Teach key stories to younger people through repetition and song.

## Safety Notes

- Obtain consent and respect cultural ownership of stories.
