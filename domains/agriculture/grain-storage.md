# Grain Storage and Pest Control

> **Domain:** agriculture | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

Grain that is stored wet or warm can lose a season's work to mould, insects,
and rodents in weeks. The two decisive variables are moisture and temperature:
dry grain below about 12-14% moisture, kept cool and sealed, stores for 6-24
months. A 1 m³ bin holds roughly 600-800 kg of grain.

## Prerequisites

- Grain cultivation and harvesting
- A dry, raised storage site

## Materials & Tools

- Raised, sealed bin or hermetic storage bags
- Moisture meter
- Clean tarps and pallets
- Diatomaceous earth
- Rodent-proof mesh
- Labels and a record book

## Procedure

### 1. Harvest and dry

1. Harvest at maturity and avoid mixing wet or mouldy grain with good grain.
2. Dry to 12-14% moisture; test with a moisture meter, not by bite.
3. Dry on clean tarps, in thin layers, with airflow and sun.
4. Cool the grain before storage; warm grain sweats as it cools.

### 2. Clean and prepare the store

1. Winnow or sieve out chaff, broken kernels, and dust.
2. Clean the bin, remove old grain, and repair holes and gaps.
3. Raise the bin off the floor and keep it away from walls.
4. Fit rodent mesh over vents and keep the lid sealed.

### 3. Fill and protect

1. Fill the bin in one session and level the surface.
2. Use hermetic bags for small quantities; seal them fully.
3. Mix diatomaceous earth at the label rate for insect control where approved.
4. Label each lot with the harvest date and moisture reading.

### 4. Monitor and treat

1. Check monthly for warmth, condensation, insects, or rodent droppings.
2. Probe the centre and bottom; a hot spot means mould or insects.
3. Remove and use affected grain first; do not mix it with clean grain.
4. Fumigate only with trained help and approved products; never in a living
   space.

## Safety Notes

- **Yellow hazard:** Mouldy grain can contain aflatoxins that cause liver
  damage and cancer. Do not eat or feed visibly mouldy grain.
- Grain dust can explode. Ventilate, avoid open flames, and wear a mask when
  cleaning.
- Fumigants are highly toxic. Use only with training, respirators, and warning
  signs.
- Never enter a grain bin that may have bridged; engulfment can kill in
  seconds.
