# Composting

> **Domain:** agriculture | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Compost converts waste into fertility. It requires four ingredients:
carbon ("browns"), nitrogen ("greens"), water, and air.

## Prerequisites

- [Soil Testing](soil-testing.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Browns: dry leaves, straw, cardboard
- Greens: kitchen scraps, grass clippings, manure
- Pitchfork
- Water

## Procedure

### Pile Construction

1. Build a 1 m³ pile.
2. Layer 3 parts browns to 1 part greens.
3. Add water until the pile feels like a wrung-out sponge.
4. Turn every 3 days for hot compost (reaches 55–65°C).
5. Ready in 4–8 weeks when cool, earthy, and crumbly.

### Sheet Composting (No-Turn)

1. Spread browns and greens directly on the soil.
2. Cover with mulch.
3. Worms and microbes do the work over 6–12 months.

### Trench Composting

1. Dig a 30 cm trench.
2. Bury kitchen scraps.
3. Cover with soil.
4. Plant above after 1 month.

### Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Smells rotten | Too wet, no air | Turn, add browns |
| Not heating up | Too dry, not enough greens | Water, add greens |
| Attracts pests | Meat/dairy in pile | Bury deep, omit these |

## Safety Notes

- Do not compost meat, dairy, or pet waste in open piles.
- Hot compost kills pathogens; cold compost does not.
- Wash hands after handling.
