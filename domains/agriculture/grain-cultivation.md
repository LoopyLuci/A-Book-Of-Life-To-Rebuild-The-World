# Grain Cultivation

## Overview

Grains (wheat, rice, maize, barley, millet) provide the calorie base of
civilization. They store dry for years.

## Prerequisites

- [Soil Testing](soil-testing.md)
- [Crop Rotation](crop-rotation.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Seed grain
- Plow or hoe
- Sickle or scythe
- Threshing surface
- Winnowing basket
- Mill (see [Mills](../materials/mills.md))

## Procedure

### Soil Preparation

1. Test soil; amend with compost to pH 6–7.
2. Plow to 15–20 cm.
3. Harrow to break clods.

### Sowing

1. Broadcast or drill seed at the rate for your grain:
   - Wheat: 100–150 kg/ha
   - Rice (transplanted): 40–60 kg/ha
   - Maize: 20–25 kg/ha
2. Cover lightly with soil.

### Growing

1. Water: 400–650 mm per season for wheat; rice needs flooding.
2. Weed at 3 weeks and 6 weeks.
3. Watch for rust, blight, and locusts.

### Harvest

1. Cut when grain is hard and heads nod.
2. Bundle into shocks; dry in the field 1–2 weeks.
3. Thresh by beating bundles on a hard surface.
4. Winnow by tossing in the wind.

### Storage

1. Dry grain to <12% moisture (bites crack, not dent).
2. Store in airtight containers with desiccant.
3. Add diatomaceous earth to prevent weevils.
4. Cool, dark, dry place. Viability: 5–20 years.

## Safety Notes

- Ergot (fungal infection of rye) is toxic — discard black grains.
- Grain dust is explosive; ventilate threshing areas.
- Rice paddies breed mosquitoes; manage standing water.
