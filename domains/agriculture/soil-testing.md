# Soil Testing

> **Domain:** agriculture | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Soil health determines crop yield. Test pH, texture, and organic matter
before planting.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Jar with lid
- Vinegar
- Baking soda
- Distilled water
- Shovel

## Procedure

### Jar Test (Texture)

1. Fill a jar ⅓ with soil, top with water, add a pinch of detergent.
2. Shake vigorously for 3 minutes.
3. Let settle 24 hours.
4. Measure layers: sand (bottom), silt, clay (top). Ratios indicate loam, sandy, or clay.

### pH Test (Vinegar & Baking Soda)

1. Place 2 tbsp soil in a cup.
2. Add vinegar. If it fizzes → alkaline (pH > 7).
3. If no fizz, repeat with baking soda + water. Fizz → acidic (pH < 7).
4. No reaction either way → neutral (pH ~7).

### Organic Matter Test

1. Weigh a dry soil sample.
2. Burn it in a fire-safe container until only ash remains.
3. Weight loss = organic matter content. Aim for 5%.

### Drainage Test

1. Dig a 30 cm hole; fill with water.
2. Refill after it drains. Measure time to drain second fill.
3. 2–4 cm/hour is ideal.

## Safety Notes

- Vinegar/baking soda reactions are harmless but messy.
- Burning soil releases smoke; do outdoors.
