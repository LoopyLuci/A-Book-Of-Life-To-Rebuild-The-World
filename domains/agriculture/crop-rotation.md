# Crop Rotation

> **Domain:** agriculture | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** green

## Overview

Rotation breaks pest cycles, balances nutrients, and prevents soil depletion.
Rule: never plant the same family in the same bed two years in a row.

## Prerequisites

- [Soil Testing](soil-testing.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Map of plots
- Seeds for 4 families

## Procedure

### The Four-Family Rotation

| Year | Bed 1 | Bed 2 | Bed 3 | Bed 4 |
|------|-------|-------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Legume | Brassica | Root | Fruiting |
| 2 | Brassica | Root | Fruiting | Legume |
| 3 | Root | Fruiting | Legume | Brassica |
| 4 | Fruiting | Legume | Brassica | Root |

Families:
- **Legume:** pea, bean, clover — fixes nitrogen
- **Brassica:** cabbage, broccoli, kale — heavy feeder
- **Root:** carrot, beet, onion — loosens soil
- **Fruiting:** tomato, pepper, squash — heavy feeder

### Cover Crops

- Winter: rye, vetch, clover
- Summer: buckwheat, cowpea
- Chop and till in before seed set to add organic matter.

### Fallow

- Rest a bed every 4 years.
- Plant with a legume cover and let it mature.

## Safety Notes

- Do not rotate tomatoes with potatoes (same family, shared diseases).
- Do not follow a legume with another legume.
