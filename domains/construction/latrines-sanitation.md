# Latrines & Sanitation

> **Domain:** construction | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

Safe excreta disposal prevents cholera, typhoid, and intestinal worms. A fly-proof latrine and a handwashing point are the highest-impact sanitation interventions in any settlement.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Shovel
- Slab or timber cover
- Vent pipe and screen
- Water and soap or ash

## Procedure

### Pit Latrine

1. Dig a pit at least 1 m deep, away from wells (>=30 m) and downhill.
2. Fit a squat slab with a tight drop hole.
3. Build a shelter for privacy and a roof to keep rain out.

### Ventilated Improved Pit

1. Run a vent pipe from the pit to above the roof.
2. Screen the pipe top to trap flies.

### Handwashing Station

1. Set a tippy-tap or bucket with a spout near the exit.
2. Provide soap or wood ash before eating and after defecation.

## Safety Notes

- Never site pits uphill or within 30 m of a water source.
- Wash hands with soap; ash is an acceptable substitute.
