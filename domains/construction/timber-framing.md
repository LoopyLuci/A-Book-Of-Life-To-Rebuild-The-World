# Timber Framing

> **Domain:** construction | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

Timber framing joins heavy members with interlocking mortise-and-tenon joints and pegs. A well-cut frame carries roof and floor loads without nails or steel.

## Prerequisites

- Simple Tools

## Materials & Tools

- Seasoned structural timber
- Hardwood pegs
- Chisels, augers, and a bow saw
- Mallet and framing square

## Procedure

### Layout

1. Square a frame on the ground using the 3-4-5 method.
2. Mark joints with a numbering system before cutting.

### Cutting Joints

1. Bore and chop mortises one-third the member's thickness.
2. Cut tenons to a snug fit; test each joint dry before assembly.

### Raising

1. Assemble bents flat, then raise them with props and pegs.
2. Join bents with girts and purlins, checking plumb and level.
3. Pin every joint with a draw-bored hardwood peg.

## Safety Notes

- Work with a spotter when raising bents; keep clear of falling members.
- Keep edged tools sheathed when not cutting.
