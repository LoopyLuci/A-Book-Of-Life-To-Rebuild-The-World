# Water Wells

> **Domain:** construction | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

A well taps groundwater for a reliable supply. The method depends on depth: dug wells for shallow aquifers, driven and bored wells for sand, and drilled wells for deep rock.

## Prerequisites

- Finding Water

## Materials & Tools

- Lining (stone, brick, or concrete rings)
- Pump or bucket hoist
- Rope and bucket

## Procedure

### Locating the Aquifer

1. Sight wells in low ground near streams, springs, or green vegetation.
2. Confirm with dowsing-free methods: test pits and local knowledge.

### Dug Well

1. Excavate a shaft 1 m wide, lining as you descend to prevent collapse.
2. Stop at the water table and line the intake with gravel.
3. Build a curb 50 cm above grade and cap to keep surface water out.

### Finishing

1. Backfill around the lining with clay to shed surface water.
2. Disinfect the well with chlorine and test before drinking.

## Safety Notes

- Dug wells can collapse or accumulate toxic gas; always have a spotter.
- Treat all well water as contaminated until disinfected.
