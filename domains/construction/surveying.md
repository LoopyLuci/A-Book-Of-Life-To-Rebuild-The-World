# Surveying

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources

source of information.*

## Overview

Surveying sets out boundaries, grades, and alignments accurately. Simple
leveling and angle measurement suffice for most community works.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Level (water or dumpy)
- Measuring chain or tape
- Range poles and pegs
- Plumb bob

## Procedure

### Leveling

1. Use a water level (clear hose) or dumpy level to transfer elevations.
2. Record backsight, foresight, and rise/fall for each station.

### Setting Out

1. Establish a baseline and square it with the 3-4-5 method.
2. Offset to locate corners and alignments.
3. Mark stations with stakes and reference ties.

## Safety Notes

- Avoid stringing lines across roads or paths without flagging.
