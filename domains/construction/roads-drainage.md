# Roads & Drainage

> **Domain:** construction | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

## Overview

All-weather roads move goods and people and connect settlements. The key is a crowned surface with side ditches so water leaves the roadbed quickly.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Gravel or crushed stone
- Hand tools and barrows
- Logs or culverts

## Procedure

### Alignment & Clearing

1. Lay out a route along ridges or valley sides, crossing streams at right angles.
2. Clear vegetation and topsoil from a 4-6 m corridor.

### Construction

1. Cut side ditches and crown the roadbed to shed water.
2. Spread and compact gravel in layers, largest stones at the base.
3. Install culverts at every drainage crossing.

## Safety Notes

- Brace trench sides during excavation.
- Keep ditches clear; a blocked ditch destroys a road in one storm.
