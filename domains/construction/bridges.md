# Bridges

> **Domain:** construction | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** red

## Overview

Bridges cross water or ravines. Span length dictates the type: simple beam for short spans, trusses for medium, and suspension for long spans.

## Prerequisites

- Timber Framing
- Rope & Cordage

## Materials & Tools

- Heavy timbers or steel
- Stone abutments
- Rope or cable
- Fasteners and pegs

## Procedure

### Surveying the Crossing

1. Measure span, bank height, and flood level.
2. Choose an abutment site on firm ground above flood stage.

### Beam Bridge

1. Build abutments of stone or concrete.
2. Lay one or more beams across, sized by span and load.
3. Deck with planks and add railings.

### Truss or Suspension

1. Assemble a truss on the bank and roll it into place.
2. For suspension, anchor cables to deadmen and hang the deck.
3. Load-test gradually before allowing traffic.

## Safety Notes

- Red hazard: structural failure is often fatal; over-build and load-test.
- Never work over moving water without fall protection and a spotter.
