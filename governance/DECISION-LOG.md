# Decision Log

Every substantive decision is recorded here. Format: `YYYY-MM-DD — Title`.

---

### 2026-01-01 — Charter adopted
**Decision:** Adopt version 1.0.0 of the governance charter.
**Context:** Initial bootstrap; ratified by founding maintainers.
**Vote:** Unanimous (5/5).

### 2026-01-15 — Domain structure ratified
**Decision:** Eight domains: survival, agriculture, materials, energy,
construction, health, knowledge, society.
**Context:** Ensures no domain becomes a dumping ground; each has a
maintainer.
**Vote:** 5/5.

### 2026-02-01 — Verification lifecycle fixed
**Decision:** Four states: draft → peer-reviewed → field-tested → deprecated.
**Context:** Prevents ambiguity in the verification ledger.
**Vote:** 5/5.

### 2026-03-01 — PNK namespace frozen
**Decision:** `pnk:` identifiers are stable and opaque; they are never
rewritten even if domains change.
**Context:** Preserves cross-KB links across forks.
**Vote:** 4/5 (one abstention).
