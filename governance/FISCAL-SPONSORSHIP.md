# Fiscal Sponsorship

A Book Of Life To Rebuild The World operates under the fiscal sponsorship of
a 501(c)(3) or equivalent nonprofit. This document records the arrangement
and the controls around it.

## Sponsor

- **Legal entity:** Open Source Collective (or equivalent)
- **Tax status:** 501(c)(3) (US) / registered charity (EU)
- **Fiscal host URL:** https://opencollective.com/a-book-of-life-to-rebuild-the-world
- **Effective date:** 2026-01-01

## Bank & payment rails

- All donations flow through the fiscal host's accounts.
- Payouts require two-of-three approval from Core Maintainers.
- No individual maintainer holds signing authority.

## Budget allocation

| Category | Target share | Notes |
|----------|-------------:|-------|
| Infrastructure | 30% | CI, hosting, domain, ZIM mirrors |
| Verification stipends | 40% | Field-test travel and materials |
| Community tooling | 15% | Translations, mobile app, SDKs |
| Reserve | 15% | Legal, audit, emergency |

## Transparency

- Financial reports published quarterly at `governance/finance/`.
- Annual audit posted to the fiscal host's public ledger.
- Any single expense over $500 requires two Core Maintainer approvals.

## Conflict of interest

- Maintainers disclose any paid relationship with vendors.
- Maintainers recuse from votes that directly benefit them financially.
- The fiscal host's COI policy applies in addition to ours.

## Dissolution clause

If the project dissolves:

1. All content is archived to the Internet Archive and Zenodo.
2. All code is archived to Software Heritage.
3. Remaining funds are donated to a like-minded open-knowledge nonprofit.
