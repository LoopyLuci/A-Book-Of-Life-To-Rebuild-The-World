# Roles & Responsibilities

## Role Matrix

| Action | Reader | Contributor | Verifier | Domain Maintainer | Core Maintainer |
|--------|:------:|:-----------:|:--------:|:-----------------:|:---------------:|
| Browse / download | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Submit PR | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Review PR | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Merge PR (own domain) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Merge PR (any domain) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| File field test | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Certify entry | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Cut release | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Amend charter | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Appoint Domain Maintainer | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Appoint Core Maintainer | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ (2/3) |

## Onboarding Path

1. **Reader → Contributor**
   - Read `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
   - Sign the CLA (automated via CLA Assistant bot).
   - Submit a first pull request (a typo fix counts).

2. **Contributor → Verifier**
   - Submit 3 merged PRs of substantive content.
   - Complete a field test using the schema.
   - Nominated by a Domain Maintainer.

3. **Verifier → Domain Maintainer**
   - 6 months of active verification.
   - Nominated by two peers.
   - Approved by consensus of Domain Maintainers for that domain.

4. **Domain Maintainer → Core Maintainer**
   - 12 months of active maintainership.
   - Nominated by a Core Maintainer.
   - Approved by 2/3 vote of Core Maintainers.

## Term Limits

- Domain Maintainers serve 12-month renewable terms.
- Core Maintainers serve 24-month renewable terms.
- No single person may hold more than one role at a time.

## Recusal

- Reviewers must recuse from entries they authored.
- Verifiers must recuse from field-testing their own entries.
- Core Maintainers must recuse from decisions that financially benefit them.
