# Beekeeping

> **Domain:** agriculture | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** yellow

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Bees pollinate ⅓ of crops and produce honey, wax, and propolis. A single
hive can yield 20–40 kg of honey per year.

## Prerequisites

- [Simple Tools](../../../../domains/survival/simple-tools.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Hive (Langstroth or top-bar)
- Smoker
- Hive tool
- Bee suit, gloves, veil
- Sugar syrup (1:1 for spring, 2:1 for fall)

## Procedure

### Catching a Swarm

1. Find a swarm on a branch in spring.
2. Shake into a box; cover quickly.
3. Transfer to a hive at dusk.

### Building a Colony

1. Install the swarm; leave alone 1 week.
2. Inspect weekly in spring/summer.
3. Feed sugar syrup until comb is drawn.

### Harvesting Honey

1. Wait until ⅔ of cells are capped.
2. Brush bees off frames; use a bee escape board.
3. Uncapp with a knife.
4. Extract with a centrifuge or crush-and-strain.
5. Leave 15 kg honey for the colony's winter.

### Winterizing

1. Reduce entrance.
2. Feed 2:1 syrup in fall.
3. Insulate; ensure ventilation to prevent condensation.

## Safety Notes

- **Yellow hazard:** Bee stings can cause anaphylaxis. Have epinephrine if allergic.
- Smoke calms but does not sedate; work slowly.
- Africanized bees are aggressive — do not work them without experience.
