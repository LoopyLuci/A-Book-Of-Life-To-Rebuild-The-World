# Field Diagnostics

> **Domain:** health | **Difficulty:** apprentice | **Safety:** yellow

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Without a laboratory, diagnosis relies on history, vital signs, and physical
examination. A structured approach separates emergencies from routine illness.

## Prerequisites

- Anatomy Basics

## Materials & Tools

- Thermometer
- Clock or watch
- Blood-pressure cuff
- Flashlight

## Procedure

### History & Vitals

1. Record onset, duration, and severity of symptoms.
2. Measure temperature, pulse, respiration, and blood pressure.

### Examination

1. Inspect, palpate, percuss, and auscultate each system.
2. Check for dehydration, pallor, and level of consciousness.

### Triage

1. Sort into immediate, delayed, and expectant categories.
2. Reassess regularly; conditions change quickly.

## Safety Notes

- Isolate patients with suspected airborne infection.
- Refer unknown or worsening cases to the most skilled caregiver available.
