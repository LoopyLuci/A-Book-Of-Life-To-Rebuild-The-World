# Measurement & Records

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Consistent measurement and record-keeping make knowledge reproducible. Standard
units, calibrated references, and ledgers underpin trade and science.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Standard weights and measures
- Ledger or logbook
- Calibration references

## Procedure

### Standards

1. Adopt units for length, mass, volume, and time.
2. Keep master references under protection.

### Records

1. Log observations with date, method, and conditions.
2. Cross-reference entries for auditability.

## Safety Notes

- Guard standards against wear and tampering.
