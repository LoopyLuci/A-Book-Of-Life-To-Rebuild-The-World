# Printing

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** journeyman | **Safety:** yellow

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Printing spreads knowledge cheaply and durably. Movable type, an oil ink, and a
screw press let a small community reproduce books and posters.

## Prerequisites

- Paper Making

## Materials & Tools

- Movable metal or wooden type
- Oil-based ink
- Screw press
- Paper

## Procedure

### Making Type

1. Carve or cast reversed letters at a uniform height.
2. Sort type into a case by frequency.

### Setting & Inking

1. Compose text in a galley, locked into a forme.
2. Ink the forme evenly with leather balls or a roller.

### Pressing

1. Lay paper, close the tympan, and run the press.
2. Hang sheets to dry; print the second side after drying.

## Safety Notes

- Ink and solvent fumes require ventilation.
- Keep hands clear of the press platen.
