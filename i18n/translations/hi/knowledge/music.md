## Overview

Music is a universal human language. It binds communities, transmits stories,
and heals trauma. Instruments can be built from local materials.

## Prerequisites

- [Simple Tools](../../../../domains/survival/simple-tools.md)

## Materials & Tools

- Wood, gut, hide, bamboo, gourd
- Metal (for strings, bells)
- Voice

## Procedure

### Rhythm First

1. Clapping patterns: 4/4, 3/4, 6/8.
2. Call and response.
3. Polyrhythms: two patterns at once.

### Drums

- **Frame drum:** hide stretched over a wooden hoop.
- **Djembe:** goblet-shaped, rope-tuned.
- **Log drum:** hollowed log with slits.

### Jaw Harp

1. Shape a metal or bamboo tongue in a frame.
2. Pluck the tongue while changing mouth shape.
3. Mouth cavity amplifies harmonics.

### Flutes

- **Bamboo flute:** cut a tube, bore finger holes, notch the mouthpiece.
- **Pan pipes:** graduated tubes bound together.
- **Ocarina:** closed vessel with finger holes.

### String Instruments

- **Berimbau:** bow with a gourd resonator.
- **Lyre:** U-shaped frame with strings.
- **Guitar:** box with neck and frets.

### Music Theory (Basic)

1. **Scale:** 7 notes per octave (do re mi fa sol la ti).
2. **Intervals:** distance between notes.
3. **Chords:** three or more notes together.
4. **Keys:** major (bright), minor (dark).

### Singing

1. Breathe from the diaphragm.
2. Open the throat.
3. Match pitch with an instrument.
4. Sing in rounds, harmonies, and unison.

### Songs for Community

- Work songs (rhythm for shared labor).
- Lullabies.
- Lamentations.
- Celebration songs.
- Anthems.

### Recording (If Available)

- Wax cylinder, magnetic tape, or digital.
- Preserve for future generations.

## Safety Notes

- Green — but loud sounds damage hearing. Protect ears around drums.
- Singing is safe; hydrate.
