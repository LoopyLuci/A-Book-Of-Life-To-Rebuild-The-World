## Overview

Law provides predictable rules for property, harm, and disputes. A simple,
published, and evenly applied code is better than an elaborate but hidden one.

## Prerequisites

- Conflict Resolution

## Materials & Tools

- Written code
- Court or hearing space
- Records

## Procedure

### Drafting

1. Define rights, duties, and offences in plain language.
2. Set proportionate penalties and fair procedures.

### Adjudication

1. Hear both sides with evidence.
2. Decide openly and record the reasoning.
3. Provide appeal to a higher body.

## Safety Notes

- Guard against bias; require recusal where a judge has a stake.
