# Library Organization

> **Domain:** knowledge | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

An organized library keeps knowledge findable for generations. A simple
classification plus a catalog protects against loss and makes retrieval fast.

## Prerequisites

- None

## Materials & Tools

- Shelving
- Catalog cards or ledger
- Labels and ink

## Procedure

### Classification

1. Group by broad subject, then by author or title.
2. Assign a stable call number to every item.

### Catalog

1. Record title, author, subject, and location for each item.
2. Maintain an index for cross-referencing.

### Preservation

1. Keep collections dry, dark, and pest-free.
2. Copy fragile items and store masters separately.

## Safety Notes

- Control humidity and fire risk; back up catalogs.
