# Field Surgery

## Overview

Field surgery addresses life-threatening injuries when evacuation is impossible.
Sterile technique, anesthesia, and hemorrhage control are mandatory
prerequisites.

## Prerequisites

- Wound Care
- Anatomy Basics

## Materials & Tools

- Sterile instruments
- Anesthetic
- Suture and needles
- Antibiotics

## Procedure

### Preparation

1. Stabilize airway, breathing, and circulation first.
2. Sterilize instruments by boiling or autoclaving.
3. Clean and drape the surgical field.

### Operate

1. Control bleeding with clamps and ligatures.
2. Debride non-viable tissue and remove foreign bodies.
3. Repair structures and close in layers where appropriate.

### Aftercare

1. Give analgesics and antibiotics.
2. Monitor for shock, infection, and bleeding.

## Safety Notes

- Red hazard: only perform when trained and when death is otherwise certain.
- Maintain sterile technique or the operation will cost more lives than it saves.
