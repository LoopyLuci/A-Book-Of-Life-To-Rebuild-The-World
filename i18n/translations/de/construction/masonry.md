# Masonry

## Overview

Masonry builds durable walls, foundations, and ovens from stone or brick bedded
in lime or cement mortar. Correct bond and footing prevent collapse.

## Prerequisites

- Lime & Cement
- Brick Making

## Materials & Tools

- Stone or fired brick
- Lime or cement mortar
- Level, trowel, and string line

## Procedure

### Footing

1. Dig a trench below frost depth and fill with rubble set in mortar.
2. Level the first course carefully; every error propagates upward.

### Laying Units

1. Spread a mortar bed and place each unit, tapping it level.
2. Stagger joints (running bond) and keep the wall plumb with a string line.
3. Rake joints when mortar is thumb-dry, then point them.

### Curing

1. Keep masonry damp for several days to prevent cracking.
2. Cover in freezing weather until mortar hardens.

## Safety Notes

- Lime and cement are caustic; wear gloves and eye protection.
- Never build tall unsupported walls; brace until cured.
