# Signaling for Rescue

> **Domain:** survival | **Difficulty:** novice | **Safety:** green

*Reference material only. Verify this information against primary sources
and qualified professionals before acting on it. This entry is not a sole
source of information.*

## Overview

Rescue teams search in patterns. The rule of three: **three of anything**
is a distress signal. Make yourself visible and audible over long distances.

## Prerequisites

- [Making Fire](../../../../domains/survival/making-fire.md) for smoke signals

## Materials & Tools

- Mirror or shiny surface
- Green vegetation (smoke)
- Whistle
- Bright cloth

## Procedure

### Signal Mirror

1. Hold mirror near eye; sight the target through a hole or notch.
2. Extend other hand toward target; adjust mirror until sunlight hits your hand.
3. Flash in groups of three.

### Smoke Signals

1. Build three fires in a triangle, 30 m apart.
2. Add green leaves or wet wood for white smoke.
3. Add oil or rubber for black smoke (contrast with sky).

### Ground-to-Air Signals

Lay out in a clearing, using contrast (dark logs on snow, light rocks on grass):

- **V** — require assistance
- **X** — require medical assistance
- **→** — traveling in this direction
- **LLL** — all well

### Audible Signals

- Whistle: 3 short blasts = distress
- Shout: 3 calls
- Bang metal on metal: 3 strikes

### Radio Distress

- VHF Channel 16 or 70 (DSC)
- Say: **MAYDAY** (voice) or send SOS in Morse (. . . – – – . . .)

## Safety Notes

- Do not ignite a signal fire in dry conditions without a firebreak.
- Do not look directly at the sun through a mirror.
- Preserve energy; signal in bursts, not continuously.
