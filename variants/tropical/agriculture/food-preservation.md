## Procedure

### Sun Drying (Tropical)

1. Slice food 3–6 mm thick.
2. Lay on a raised rack, 1 m off the ground.
3. Cover with mosquito netting.
4. Dry in full sun 2–4 days, turning daily.
5. **Finish in a solar dryer** to prevent mold during the humid season.

### Salting (Tropical)

1. Layer food with coarse salt (1 kg per 4 kg food).
2. Weight down; keep submerged.
3. Ready in 1–3 weeks.
4. Rinse before eating; soak if too salty.

### Smoking (Tropical)

1. Build a smokehouse elevated 1 m above ground.
2. Use coconut husk, hardwoods, or citrus wood.
3. Cold smoke (30–40°C) for 2–4 days.
4. Hot smoke (60–80°C) for immediate consumption.

### Fermenting (Tropical)

1. Submerge vegetables in 2% brine.
2. Add chili, garlic, or ginger as antimicrobials.
3. Ferment 3–7 days at 28–32°C.
4. Refrigerate or keep in a cool earthen pit.

### Root Cellaring (Tropical)

1. Use an underground pit lined with banana leaves.
2. Keep cool with evening air exchange.
3. Store taro, yam, cassava, sweet potato.
4. Check weekly for rot.

## Safety Notes

- **High humidity accelerates mold.** Use solar dryers or smoke.
- **Botulism risk from fermented low-acid foods.** Boil before eating.
- **Cassava must be thoroughly cooked** — raw cassava contains cyanide.
