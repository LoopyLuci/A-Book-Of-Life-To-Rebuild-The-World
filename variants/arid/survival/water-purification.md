## Procedure

### Method 1: Solar Still (Preferred in Arid Climates)

1. Dig a pit 1 m wide, 60 cm deep.
2. Place a collection cup in the center.
3. Fill the pit with green vegetation or urine-soaked cloth.
4. Cover with clear plastic; seal edges with soil.
5. Place a small stone at the center above the cup.
6. Collect condensed water after 6–8 hours.
7. Yield: 0.5–1.5 L per day per still.

### Method 2: Transpiration Bag

1. Tie a clear plastic bag around a leafy branch of a non-toxic tree.
2. Seal the opening; weight the bottom corner.
3. Collect transpired water after a day.
4. Yield: 100–300 mL per bag per day.

### Method 3: Atmospheric Water Generator (Improvised)

1. Bury a metal drum with a small opening above ground.
2. Fill with rocks heated during the day.
3. At night, cool air condenses on the rocks.
4. Collect water at the bottom.

## Safety Notes

- **Do not dig in dry riverbeds during flash flood season.**
- **Never drink urine directly** — purify it first through a still.
- Locate water in rock crevices, shaded cliffs, and dry stream beds on the
  outside of bends.
- Watch for bees and ants at dusk — they lead to water.
