## Procedure

### Snow Cave (Preferred in Arctic)

1. Find a drift at least 2 m deep.
2. Dig a tunnel 1 m long, then up into a dome.
3. Smooth the ceiling to prevent dripping.
4. Poke a ventilation hole with a ski pole.
5. Build a sleeping platform 30 cm above the floor.
6. Line the floor with pine boughs or a sleeping pad.

### Quinzhee (Snow Pile Shelter)

1. Pile snow 2 m high.
2. Let it sinter for 2 hours.
3. Hollow out the interior, leaving 30 cm walls.
4. Cut a door below the sleeping platform.
5. Ventilate at the top.

### Igloo (Block Snow)

1. Cut snow blocks 60 × 30 × 15 cm.
2. Lay in a spiral, canting inward.
3. Cut a door at the base.
4. Seal cracks with loose snow.

### Tarp Shelter (Forest)

1. Ridge line between two trees.
2. Tarp draped over, secured with stakes.
3. Windbreak wall on the windward side.
4. Fire reflector wall 1 m away.

## Safety Notes

- **Ventilation is mandatory.** CO₂ builds up rapidly in sealed snow shelters.
- Do not sleep in snow caves during thaw.
- Keep clothing dry; sweat kills.
- Eat before sleeping to fuel metabolism.
